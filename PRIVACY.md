# Privacy Policy

Kode24 Ad Blocker runs locally in your browser and only modifies webpage content on kode24.no to remove ads and commercial elements.

## Data Collection

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any data.

## Personal Information

This extension does not access or handle personal information.

## Tracking and Analytics

This extension does not include analytics, tracking, or external network requests.
